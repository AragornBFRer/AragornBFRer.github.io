Yuqi Yang (Aragorn)

I study statistical methods for reliable AI, with a focus on conformal inference, multiple testing, distribution shift, and rigorous evaluation of modern ML systems.

I am completing a bachelor's degree at HKUST, double majoring in Mathematics and Computer Science. I currently work with Prof. Ying Jin at Wharton and Prof. Xinzhou Guo at HKUST. Previously, I worked on cognitive science and intention understanding with Prof. Yangqiu Song at the KnowComp Group, and visited ETH Zurich in Spring 2025.

Research

  • Uncertainty and robustness. Conformal prediction, reliable coverage, and deployment under distribution shift.
  • Evaluation of AI systems. Statistically rigorous evaluation, falsification, verification, and multiple testing.
  • Foundations. Statistical foundations of modern AI/ML, with active interests in causal inference and deep learning theory.
