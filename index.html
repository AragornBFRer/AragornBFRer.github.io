I study statistics and AI, with a current focus on uncertainty quantification, and statistics-guided agentic systems.

I am pursuing a bachelor's degree at HKUST, majoring in Mathematics and Computer Science. I will start a PhD in Statistics at the Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania, in Fall 2026. I currently work with Prof. Ying Jin at Wharton and Prof. Xinzhou Guo at HKUST. Previously, I worked on cognitive science and intention understanding with Prof. Yangqiu Song at the KnowComp Group, and visited ETH Zurich in Spring 2025.

During my undergrad, I also did internships in quantitative research at Hangzhou Wangdao, hedge fund research at Jihai Investment, and software development at Hundsun.
